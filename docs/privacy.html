Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 June 2026

Portfolio Tracker Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal stock portfolio tracking tool for Indian (NSE/BSE) and US (NYSE/NASDAQ) markets. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights over it. By using this app, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary
We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We collect only what is needed to provide the service — your email address, portfolio holdings, and payment status.

1 Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name (provided when you sign in via Google or email/password through Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile photo URL if you use Google Sign-In (fetched from your Google account).

Portfolio data

  • Stock tickers, quantities, and average buy prices you enter into the app.
  • This data is stored in Google Firestore under your user ID and is never shared with other users.

Payment information

  • Subscription plan chosen (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime).
  • Razorpay order ID and payment ID (reference numbers only — we never see or store your card or bank details).
  • Subscription status and expiry date stored in Firestore.

Technical information

  • Browser type and operating system (collected automatically by Firebase Hosting and Firebase Analytics if enabled).
  • IP address (handled by Google's infrastructure; not stored by us directly).
  • Error logs from Firebase Cloud Functions (retained for up to 30 days by Google Cloud Logging).

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and protect access to your personal portfolio data.
  • To display your holdings, calculate P&L, and deliver stock alerts.
  • To process subscription payments via Razorpay and unlock Pro features.
  • To send you Telegram alerts (only if you configure a Telegram bot token).
  • To diagnose errors and improve the reliability of the service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

3 Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

Firebase (Google LLC)
Authentication, Firestore database, and hosting. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.
Payment processing for subscriptions. Razorpay handles all card/UPI/banking data on their PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. We receive only a payment reference ID. razorpay.com/privacy
Yahoo Finance (yfinance)
Live and historical stock price data. No personal data is sent to Yahoo Finance. Prices are fetched server-side or directly in your browser. Yahoo Privacy Policy
Telegram (optional)
If you choose to configure Telegram alerts, your Telegram chat ID is stored locally in the agent's config file on your own machine. It is never sent to our servers.

4 Data Storage and Security

  • All user data is stored in Google Firestore (region: us-central1), protected by Firebase Security Rules that ensure each user can only access their own data.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+.
  • Payment credentials (Razorpay Key Secret) are stored in Firebase Cloud Functions environment variables and are never exposed to the browser.
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication.
Important
While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password for your Google account and enable 2-factor authentication.

5 Data Retention

  • Account and portfolio data — retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Payment records — Razorpay order and payment IDs are retained for 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations.
  • Server logs — Cloud Function logs are retained for 30 days by Google Cloud Logging.

6 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update your email or display name through your Google account settings.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated portfolio data. Contact us at the email below.
  • Data portability — export your portfolio holdings at any time from within the app.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at rrk822026@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Cookies and Local Storage

  • Firebase Authentication uses browser local storage and session cookies to keep you signed in.
  • We do not use third-party advertising cookies.
  • You can clear these at any time through your browser settings, which will sign you out of the app.

8 Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify active users via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
